# open-course pages (Syllabus, Handout, Slides)

Mode: Read. Surfaces: `index.html` (Syllabus), `lessons/<id>/handout.html`, `lessons/<id>/slides.html`, all generated by `skills/learning/open-course/scripts/build-course.mjs` from `assets/course.css`, `assets/progress.js`, `assets/slides.js`, `assets/rail.woff2`.

Audience and job: learners reading a Handout for a long stretch on desktop or phone and checking claims against Sources; teachers projecting Slides. Courses run to 30+ Lessons, so the Syllabus must never be one long scroll to find a Lesson.

Constraints: single offline HTML file per page, nothing fetched; Exercise answers fold without script; Slides read without script; dark mode first-class; zh-TW on Windows is the release check.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Course is a strip of film on a cutting bench: every Lesson is a numbered frame on one perforated rail, and a folded tape flag marks where you stopped. Refuses the course-platform card grid and the long document scroll.

OWN-WORLD: Bench black ground (#0c0b0a) owns the frame on every page in both modes; grain-orange (#e8620c) rail with sprocket perforations; reading happens only in punched paper windows (#f5f1ea light, #1c1916 dark). One condensed grotesk (embedded Barlow Condensed 600) for every numeral and Latin label; CJK in the system sans. State is a mark, not a hue: paper cell = here, ink cell = opened before, outline = not yet.

STORY: The reader sees the whole Course as one rail, jumps to any Lesson in one click, reads calmly in the paper window, follows a citation to its Source, and moves to the next frame.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Syllabus at 1440: bench masthead with the Course title large in paper ink and its facts beside it; directly beneath, the full-width orange rail with every Lesson as a numbered cell (30 fit one row), the tape flag folded over the last opened one; the paper window with the two-column frame list starts above the fold. Primary action: a rail cell or a Lesson title.

FORM: Film cutting bench select rail (catalog challenger operate-a-cutting-bench-select-rail), chosen by the user over the assigned scholarly-journal direction (position 7 on the grounded list); seed: seed:d8ffb4ae10a6feb6

Signature interaction: the rail. On the Syllabus, Handout header, and Slides footer it is the same perforated strip; on Slides its cells are the slides and the flag follows the current one.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
